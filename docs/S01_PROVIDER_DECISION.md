## Decision criteria

A provider is not selected from benchmark claims alone. The owner must listen to the same original Mandarin samples and score:
- warmth and long-session comfort;
- natural phrasing, pauses and prosody;
- Chinese pronunciation and names;
- restrained dialogue/emotion;
- consistency across chunk boundaries;
- artifacts, latency and reliability;
- full-book cost at the actual billing unit;
- commercial rights, privacy, retention and regional processing.
